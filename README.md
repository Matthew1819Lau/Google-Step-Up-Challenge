## What is the project about :question:
Project name: DIGDATA X GOOGLE – GEMINI PRO MARKETING STRATEGY 

- Developed a data-driven multi-channel marketing strategy to increase adoption of Gemini Pro among university students across four regions (United Kingdom 🇬🇧, Germany :de:, Egypt :egypt: and Saudi Arabia :united_arab_emirates:) within $10M USD dollars and 8 weeks timeframe.
- Analysed 3 datasets (i) Historic Campaign Data, (ii) Brand Lift Study, (iii) Creative Performance Report to solve the questions in the Data Science Route and the Data Strategist Route resepectively.

## How to handle the problems :interrobang:
  - Joinned tables to find the connection of some features and found out the target values in a complicated datasets by using VLookup Excel function.
  - Applied statistic, markteing strategy and data science techniques, such as A/B testing, P-value, Z-test and data preprocessing to find meaningful insights.
  - Identified student had higher purchase intent during exam periods and stronger adoption in non-European markets.
  - Visualised the result by using Jupyter notebook.
